# KrishiSetu AI (कृषि सेतु) - Project Overview

> **Smart Farming, Brighter Futures**  
> An integrated agricultural intelligence and commerce platform built to empower Indian farmers with real-time agronomic insights, price discovery, and direct market access.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

KrishiSetu AI bridges the critical gaps between agricultural technology, localized agronomy, and market economics. By unifying four essential pillars into a cohesive, farmer-friendly dashboard, the platform enables farmers to make informed decisions that enhance crop health, conserve water resources, and optimize market profits.

The platform is designed around four specialized modules:
1. **Crop Disease Detection**: Instant AI-assisted visual diagnosis of leaf afflictions with localized treatment guidance.
2. **Mandi Price Intelligence**: Multi-market price transparency, historical trends, and modal price comparisons across regional APMCs.
3. **Smart Irrigation Advisor**: Stage-specific, weather-integrated water management recommendations to conserve groundwater and boost crop yield.
4. **Farmer Marketplace**: Direct B2B/B2C marketplace connecting farmers directly to retail and wholesale buyers, cutting out unnecessary intermediaries.

---

## 2. Core Value Propositions

| Dimension | Challenge Faced by Farmers | KrishiSetu AI Solution |
|---|---|---|
| **Crop Health** | Delayed diagnosis of fungal/bacterial blight leading to crop loss | Instant on-device/cloud photo analysis with confidence scoring and treatment protocols |
| **Market Rates** | Information asymmetry; middleman exploitation at physical mandis | Real-time modal prices and trends across neighboring mandis (Jaipur, Sanganer, Phagi, Chomu) |
| **Irrigation** | Over-watering or under-watering due to unpredictable weather | 5-day weather-adjusted irrigation scheduling tailored to soil type and crop phenology |
| **Marketplace** | Inability to reach competitive buyers directly | Direct digital listings with harvest dates, grade categorization, and buyer inquiry matching |

---

## 3. Product Vision & Principles

1. **Farmer-First UX**: Clean, high-contrast layouts, visual iconography, regional language readiness, and minimal technical jargon.
2. **Data Honesty**: Zero fabrication. Placeholders clearly indicate mock status until authenticated APMC APIs or weather feeds are wired up.
3. **Modular Autonomy**: Modular App Router architecture allowing specialized engineers to build their features independently without stepping on shared infrastructure.
4. **Resilience & Offline Grace**: The dashboard interface functions completely without external database keys, degrading gracefully with local state and sample telemetry.
